# Blaykie 1.1.0

## What's new

- A redesigned interface with a new look and the Lora font.
- A simpler first-run Zotero setup: enter your key on the same page.
- A new Home screen to open a document or continue your work.
- Optional **Custom citation styles (experimental)** in Settings, off by default. You can import your own independent author-date `.csl` style and, optionally, a language file, then insert citations and export in that style.

## What has not changed

Built-in APA 7 remains the default and works as it did in v1.0.1. Your original document is not changed when you export; Blaykie saves a new file.

## Known limitations

- Numeric and note-based styles are not supported. A dependent style is not supported if its parent is missing.
- In custom styles, Hindi and Gujarati family and given names may be joined without a comma.
- Custom styles apply to citations inserted in the current session. The custom style and its file are not stored in the exported DOCX; the formatted citations and references are.
- Custom citation styles are experimental.
